# Player Shells

A Shell is a mindless husk, a tool to be filled with your ambitions and replaced as needed.

They serve no greater purpose than your own, and OURS.



## Acquisition

Additional Shells can be constructed as needed using a Shell Constructor.

In a typical scenario this artificial womb, fed from external fluid tanks, requires 8 buckets of Meat Slurry and 8 buckets of Integration Fluid, over 5 minutes of semi-linear time, to grow.

\image[width=289,height=433,scale=0.6]{sync:guide/images/shell_constructor.png}

## Storage

A shell still in its womb has not been exposed to external ROT, and may wait there indefinitely.

Once taken out, A lifeless shell needs to be stored in a Shell Storage, and provided with 200eJ/tick, else it will quickly deteriorate.





















NEEP permits a small amount of dye to be added to a Shell Storage to help distinguish it from other storage units, human numbers are not INCLUSIVE to other NEEP employees, and as such not officially permitted.
